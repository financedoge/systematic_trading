How the modes are identified

Regression corridors: slope of log price, scaled by ATR, with fit quality. Range has its own flatness, width, efficiency and confirmation thresholds.

Channel structure: compare the early and late thirds of the window for rising/falling lower and upper price quantiles. No future-confirmed turning points.

Moving-average corridors: fast versus slow average log price, the slow average's direction and directional price efficiency.

Volume version: each directional detector has its own signed price-volume pressure and activity thresholds. The range detector has its own activity ceiling. Volume is measured relative to its prior21-session median, with extreme ratios capped.

Separate blocks: range, up and down have different fitted lengths and thresholds. Short-domain windows are10/21/42/63 sessions; long-domain windows63/126/189/252. The blocks are jointly scored on training trading outcomes, not hindsight labels.

An intact, previously identified range box takes priority over a directional reading on another timescale. Conflicting directions need a25% strength/quality advantage; absent or unresolved evidence retains the last confirmed mode. Initial unknown→up does not buy. The primary strategy has no TP, stop-loss, time exit or cooldown override. All decisions use completed closes and execute later; a large loss can occur before down is confirmed.

What is and is not established

The audited published input batch is unchanged: adjusted OHLC for returns and raw volume for activity. No provider downloads or source substitution. The supported pool is retrospective; historical membership and publication vintage are not certified. Both chronological periods have been inspected in earlier research, so they are development evidence, not untouched holdouts.

Alpha controls for the same ETF and BIL; IC21 relates the desired long-position state to subsequent21-session excess return.

Predeclared comparisons

Fixed: jointly fit a fast and slow horizon. A fast up signal inside slow down is either blocked or requires a stronger price breakout. Fresh fast down exits without an old slow-up veto.

Adaptive: choose the fast window from 10/21/42/63 sessions and the slow window from 63/126/189/252, using trailing completed one-step forecasting errors and fit quality. A five-session dwell and fitted improvement margin reduce window churning. Window lengths are adaptive within declared banks, not unrestricted.

Protected / stress: add monotone trailing protection, tighter for countertrend positions. The stress version tightens further on rising relative volatility; its volume variant also responds to unusual volume. Stops cannot widen as volatility rises.

HMM: separate three-state fast and slow Gaussian models observe all horizons simultaneously, with or without volume features. Their probabilities are combined at the trading decision. This is not a jointly fitted factorial HMM and does not make window length a hidden state.

Training estimates state emissions, persistence and transitions. State names require directional training-centroid checks; unidentified states stay unknown. Trading uses only forward-filtered probabilities. Backward smoothing is used inside training estimation, never to relabel historical trading decisions.

HMM alone, HMM with protection and HMM with stress tightening are separate comparisons. Models remain fixed during each evaluation period. HMM calibration scores are in-sample, not a rolling model-refit validation.

Execution: protection is tested against the completed close and fills at the next open, or one extra session in delay tests. The red level is not an intraday order or guaranteed loss cap. A newly tightened level applies to the following close. Entry levels use actual fills and previously known ATR. New entries require a fresh eligibility transition.

448 candidates per ETF/cutoff: 32 matched parameter vectors × seven components × price/volume. Top three per family undergo all six cost/delay tests. Method and parameter selection use training only; no fallback conceals a poor new result. Both evaluation periods are previously inspected development history.

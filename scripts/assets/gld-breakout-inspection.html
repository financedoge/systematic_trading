Data, execution and verification

Original GLD parameters were fitted on pre-2023 data and are unchanged here. The short and long fits have different structural search domains. The corrected engine counts the crossing close toward confirmation, applies the first entry-close stop check, respects cooldown and uses symmetric order timing. ATR for initial risk is frozen at the confirmation close. Cash earns zero; fractional units and 5 bp per side apply.

Daily adjusted OHLC and audited raw volume are from the published batch pinned in the fitted-signal audit. This page reconstructs all stop components and checks them against the corrected simulator to numerical precision. Range quantiles exclude the breakout day; fills match actual next-session opens. Daily display/export values are rounded to seven decimals only after validation.

Auditing prices does not establish historical publication availability. Previously inspected 2023 onward results are development evidence, not an untouched holdout. This report does not change a running strategy or place any order.

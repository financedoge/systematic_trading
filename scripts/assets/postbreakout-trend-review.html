The precise decision rule

Before a rising trend forms: retain the ETF's fitted initial protective stop and failed-breakout time exit. Entry, breakout confirmation, volume rules, cooldown and continuation stay unchanged.

After at least five post-entry closes: fit a rising line to log prices using only closes through yesterday. Project its lower residual boundary forward and subtract an allowance for normal price fluctuations. Only a positive fitted slope creates new support.

Once established: the effective exit floor is the greater of initial protection and the rising support. The support can ratchet upward, never downward. A close below the floor triggers a sale at the next open, or one session later in the delay test.

Individual fitting: each ETF separately chooses a maximum lookback of 10/21/42/63 sessions and a buffer of 0.5/1/2 ATR, or retains its existing exit. ATR measures typical recent daily price movement. A fixed 21-session / 1 ATR control tests a common rule without individual selection.

This isolates the exit. It does not add downward-channel breakout entries or select ETFs from their later results. The line excludes today's close; no future turning points, intraday fills or guaranteed stop prices. All tests are on previously inspected historical periods.

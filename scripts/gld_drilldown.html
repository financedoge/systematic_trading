GLD: from range to entry, one day at a time

This study now uses daily trading decisions. Every completed close evaluates the range, confirmation and exit rules. A changed target executes at the next trading session’s open. There is no calendar rebalancing. This first drill-down keeps the numerical parameters fixed so the effect of the execution change is isolated.

The holding rule is the immediate problem. In 2024 onward, the daily strategy entered seven confirmed breakouts. Six ended on the five-session progress test. Those exits were not caused by stop losses or profit-taking. The remaining position’s trailing exit has never activated.

Stops below are close-based conditions, followed by a next-open exit. They are not intraday stop orders or guaranteed fill prices. As of October 16, 2026, the effective stop was $357.45 versus a $382.01 close. Trailing activation required a $431.98 peak; the recorded peak was only $428.07. There is no fixed take-profit target.

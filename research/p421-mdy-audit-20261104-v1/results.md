# Why five MDY price histories are under review

**Repair how the database identifies securities and records corporate actions. Defer a broad historical price backfill.** Four of the five largest MDY holdings under review already have all 253 required sessions under their December 2024 tickers. For the fifth, Pure Storage, the observations exist under a later ticker. The five account for 3.57% of MDY's filed common-equity value. Qualifying all five would lift the pilot's diagnostic coverage from 17.92% to 21.49%. Broader historical data needs cannot be estimated from five deliberately selected large positions.

## Which records failed

The denominator is $24.276 billion across all 401 filed equities. The 253-session window runs from February 27, 2024 to the February 28, 2025 close for the March 3 monthly decision. The latest published stock refresh used a single provider and explicitly carried forward unresolved reviews from the September multi-source audit. Its current conflict counter alone omits that earlier evidence. The price count below is the earlier audit's count of distinct dates flagged against its primary source. **What a complete window establishes.** For WSM, ILMN, EME and IBKR, every session has positive, finite raw and adjusted open, high, low and close, and raw volume. The September and November publications agree exactly on all four raw prices and volume on all 253 dates for each name. The largest difference in adjacent adjusted-close log returns across those vintages is below 0.005 basis points. The comparison records covering these dates all derive from Yahoo. Their agreement tests stability across captures. It leaves independent source accuracy, historical availability and security continuity unresolved. The earlier price-conflict dates and the comparison intervals for all twelve volume failures lie outside the pilot window. ## Two corporate actions and a reused ticker

**EMCOR provides a concrete repair using existing bars.** For December 13–14, 2012, we inferred the cash adjustment from the change in adjusted-close to close ratios. The 2020 archive implies $0.060005 per share. Two September 2026 captures imply $0.309976 and $0.309996. The published action ledger records $0.31, and the company's annual report confirms a $0.25 special and $0.06 regular payment. The older archive therefore has a specific adjustment break. Record the supporting evidence against that source and interval, then rerun the adjustment comparison before retiring the flag. Other volume differences need their own disposition. [EMCOR 2012 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/105634/000010563413000042/eme-20121231x10k.htm).

**Illumina needs distinct fields for the economic event and the feed's adjustment.** Shareholders kept their Illumina shares and received one GRAIL share per six Illumina shares. The ex-date was June 25, 2024. The provider labels that date as a 1.028 split, and the publisher uses that factor to reconstruct raw prices and share volume for 82 earlier sessions in the pilot. A check against a pre-event 2020 Yahoo vintage on 315 common sessions supports keeping the numerical correction under investigation. Before reversal the median volume ratio is 1.028. After reversal it is 1.000, with a maximum difference of 0.037%. Removing the factor on the strength of the event label alone would discard evidence. Keep the distribution terms, the provider's numerical adjustment, and independently verified price/volume treatment separate. [Illumina 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000119312524167025/d851030d8k.htm) · [Nasdaq corporate action notice](https://nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ECA2024-262).

**Pure Storage needs an effective-dated identity record.** MDY filed Class A shares under PSTG, CUSIP 74624M102. OCC identifies the same CUSIP for the change to P effective April 17, 2026. The old PSTG and candidate P series have identical adjusted closes over all 1,130 overlapping sessions from October 6, 2015 through April 1, 2020. The candidate's audit separately records the name Pandora Media and 1,082 source observations preceding its listing boundary. A global ticker alias could bring in that unrelated history. Retain PSTG as the ticker observed in 2024, record the class, CUSIP, exchange, effective dates and evidence publication dates, and verify the candidate bars against that security before publication. The 2026 change is evidence for a retrospective identity audit. A March 2025 strategy could not have used the announcement. ## Database decision

1. **Fix the reconciliation record.** Identify the instrument, source pair, price or volume field, affected dates, evidence, and disposition. Preserve lifetime reviews while testing whether a specific window can be certified for a specific use. Retain the current review flags until the evidence supports a versioned publication.
2. **Expand the evidence selectively.** Add dated ticker ownership, share-class and listing history, and independently documented action terms. For unresolved prices or volume, acquire independent observations for the affected dates and fields. Repeated downloads from the same provider cannot establish an independent comparison.
3. **Size the remaining work before a bulk purchase or backfill.** The proposed next item is to classify the other 320 MDY review positions by equity weight, missing sessions, earlier conflicts, action issues and identity gaps. Include the nine unavailable names as a separate group. Their gaps may differ from the five reviewed here. The existing pilot and constituent-alpha replay remain at their prior status.
